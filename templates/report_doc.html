{# The report document — V2. What changed from V1 is the structure and the voice, not the look. The typography, the palette, the bordered cards, the dark masthead rule and the running foot are V1's, unchanged: this is the same document Acre Insights was already sending, reorganised the way Cathy asked for. What changed: * Three pages instead of seven — summary, map, detailed findings. * No 0-100 field health score. Plain counts: areas marked, acres, category. * Fifteen near-identical zones are stated once as a pattern on page 1, and still listed individually and in full on page 3. * It informs rather than instructs. No ranked list of orders. Everything page 1 says is assembled in patterns.py from the agronomist's own observation, likely-cause and recommendation text. Nothing on it introduces a cause, a diagnosis or a recommendation the annotations do not contain. Screen, print and PDF --------------------- On a wide screen and on paper this is composed as real A4 sheets: each .sheet is exactly 210 x 297 mm, the page box has no margin of its own, and what sits on one sheet on screen sits on one page on paper. Below 820px it reflows into a single readable column. The earlier build scaled the whole sheet down instead, which fitted a phone at 44% and left the body text at about four pixels — technically the same document, practically unreadable, and the farmer reading this on a handset in a field is the realistic case. The content and its order are identical; only the columns reflow, and Download PDF is always the exact sheets.
